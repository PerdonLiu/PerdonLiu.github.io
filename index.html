Senior Embodied AI Researcher JD Explore Academy Shenzhen, China

Hi! I am Peidong Liu, a senior embodied AI researcher at JD Explore Academy. My research focuses on pre-training for embodied intelligence, with an emphasis on learning generalizable action representations and policies from large-scale heterogeneous data, including human demonstrations, simulation data, and data collected through embodiment-agnostic interfaces such as UMI. I aim to transfer these capabilities to downstream robotic applications and use real-world robot data to continually refine models, establishing a closed-loop learning framework that integrates pre-training, deployment, and iterative improvement. If you are interested in an internship opportunity, please email me.

Previously, I led the VLA Team at DJI Automotive (now Zhuoyu Technology), focusing on fine-tuning VLA models to handle long-tail driving scenarios. I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Tsinghua University in 2022 as an Outstanding Graduate, and my B.S. from Sun Yat-sen University in 2019, graduating summa cum laude. I have also interned at SenseTime and WeChat.
